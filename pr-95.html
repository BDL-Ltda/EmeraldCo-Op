The vanilla trade screens, over the internet, with a clean way out

While a co-op trade session runs, the link-cable calls of trade.c travel ROM to ROM through the mailbox and trade_link. Big picture

The ROMs talk; Lua only relays

The #80 spike showed the vanilla trade screens work over a block-level adapter. This PR is the production version of that adapter. link.c routes its block, standby and close functions to src/coop/virtual_link.c only during a session. Each ROM sends COOP_MSG_LINK (type 5) messages through the existing multi-slot mailbox; client/lib/virtuallink.lua turns them into trade_link messages for the partner, and back. The server is unchanged: it still broadcasts, and receivers filter by targetId.

How it works

A handshake that tolerates uneven starts

Lua sends its own ROM a START with the role (the lower player ID leads) and the partner's ID. Each ROM fills its LinkPlayer with the vanilla LocalLinkPlayerToBlock and sends HELLO every second until it has the partner's HELLO and an acknowledgment of its own. Packets sent before the other Lua relays anything are just lost and repeated. Then both open the vanilla trade menu. From there on trade.c drives everything; a 1-byte KEEPALIVE goes out after 60 quiet frames.

Timeout and cancel

Five ways to notice the partner is gone, one way out

600 frames (10 s) without any packet from the partner, a CANCEL from Lua (the partner's leave, a sequence gap, a full queue), the partner's ABORT, a full ROM queue, or a cleared lua_connected flag (our socket or the server is gone). Before the save starts, all of them lead to the same exit.

The summary and evolution screens own memory trade.c doesn't track, so a cancel waits until they hand back to a trade screen. TryLeaveCoopLinkTrade leaves the menu screens (after state 0 allocated them) and the animation screens up to CB2_SaveAndEndTrade state 1, mirroring the frees in CB_ExitCanceledTrade and CB2_FreeTradeAnim.

Under the hood

The ten packet kinds

Every COOP_MSG_LINK message starts with a kind byte, from include/coop/virtual_link.h. Click one. "Peer" packets go ROM to ROM; the local ones never leave the machine, and Lua drops them if a partner sends them.

Decisions in this PR

Three choices worth checking

The cancel line is the first save barrier

// trade.c, CB2_SaveAndEndTrade
case 1:
CoopLink_OnSaveStart();
SetTradeLinkStandbyCallback(0);
Why

The partner can't pass its first save barrier without ours, so before it nobody has saved and undoing is safe. After it, canceling could leave one side saved; instead the barriers are released and the local save finishes, so nothing hangs. It is not an atomic distributed save; recovery stays in #83.

The ROM owns the timeout, in frames

#define COOP_LINK_TIMEOUT_FRAMES (10 * 60)
#define COOP_LINK_KEEPALIVE_FRAMES 60
Why

Keeps Lua a dumb pipe (architecture.md) and catches every failure the same way, including a dead script or a paused emulator. The cost: fast-forward shortens it in real time; documented.

Undo with a snapshot, not a reload

// taken at START, restored on cancel
party, partyCount, mail, pokedex,
seen1, seen2, giftRibbons, gameStats
Why

Everything trade.c can change before the save: TradeMons, mail, Pokédex flags, gift ribbons at menu creation, and the evolution's game stat. Reloading the save would also throw away the player's unsaved progress.
