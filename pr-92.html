Big picture

The attendant owns the place; Lua owns the invitation

Issue #82 chooses the Pokémon Center 2F counter as the entry point. All towns share the Direct Corner script. The new Co-op trade option keeps the original Link services path and vanilla eligibility checks.

How it works

Availability is separate from overworld presence

Waiting players appear even in different towns. Entering an invitation marks both peers unavailable. A third player receives the relay broadcast but cannot act on a message addressed to someone else.

The picture omits the availability messages around each transition. Decline, timeout and partner cancellation return to waiting; B on the waiting list closes the native task. Socket loss releases its script.

Under the hood

Explore the states and the retry barrier

Values come from client/lib/trade.lua. The native snapshot echoes a 16-bit visit token, state, notice, partner ID and at most four selectable IDs.

Decisions

Keep the invitation bounded and recoverable

Reuse vanilla eligibility

call CableClub_EventScript_CheckPartyTradeRequirements
Why

The issue requires vanilla rules. The shared script retains Pokédex and Bad Egg gates and checks two Pokémon and Enigma Berry restrictions before entering the new UI.

Drain old replies before retrying

retiring[partner] = true
Why

Protocol v2 has no invitation nonce. A terminal reply forms a FIFO barrier so an old accept cannot accept a new invitation. An active partner acknowledges once; unmatched cancels are ignored. Crossing invites keep the smaller ID as inviter.

Share the existing transport budget

client:sendTrade(message)
Why

The network clock ticks once per frame; events and ROM commands reuse it. Types 7/8 leave 5/6 to the parallel #94 spike, whose fork branch still requires a source merge and fresh build when integrated. Controls share the 20-message budget with ping and presence. Sixteen unsent controls are the bound; overflow closes the socket so leave removes stale availability. Native cancellation retries a full ring. No new socket, address or dependency is needed.
